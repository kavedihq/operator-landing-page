User guide

Everything Kavedi does, and how to set it up. Updated October 7, 2026
The short version
  • Connect the WhatsApp number you already use. It takes about two minutes.
  • Teach Kavedi your answers once, in your own words.
  • Switch on agents. Each one does one job: answering questions, booking, reminding, checking in.
  • Anything an agent doesn’t know comes to your Inbox. Kavedi never makes up an answer.

Getting started

  1. Sign in at kavedi.com/sign-in with Google or your email. During the beta your email has to be let in first. Apply here.
  2. The welcome. The first time you sign in, Kavedi asks your name or your business’s, what you do, and the questions people ask you most. Each step can be skipped.
  3. Connect WhatsApp. Enter your number with its country code. Kavedi shows an 8 character pairing code. On your phone open WhatsApp, then Settings, Linked devices, Link a device, Link with phone number instead, and type the code. Your phone keeps working exactly as before.
  4. The setup guide (Setup at the top on a computer, a card on Today on a phone) shows what’s left: teach an answer, fill in About you, switch on an agent.

Kavedi links to your number like WhatsApp Web does. It isn’t WhatsApp or Meta, and it never needs your WhatsApp password.

Teaching Kavedi

Everything agents say comes from what you teach them. Go to Knowledge.

  • The teach box. Type or say it the way you would to a new member of staff: “A cut is $40. Braids start at $120. We’re closed on Sundays.” Kavedi turns it into answers, each with the question people ask and other ways they ask it, sorted into topics. If something disagrees with an answer you already have, it asks which one to keep.
  • Upload a file. A price list, flyer, timetable, PDF, Word document or photo of a menu. Kavedi reads it into answers for you to check. The file itself isn’t kept.
  • Questions it couldn’t answer are listed at the top. Answer one and it is sent to everyone who asked, as a reply to what they asked.
  • Try it shows what Kavedi would reply to a message, without sending anything.

About you (Settings) matters too: your name, what you do, address, phone and website go with every answer, so “where are you?” is always right.

Agents

An agent is a worker with one job. It has a start (when it takes a message), steps (what it does), what it knows, and rules. Four come with every account:

AgentIts jobStarts
ReceptionistAnswers questions from what you taught itAny message no other agent took
BookerBooks, moves and cancels appointmentsWhen someone wants a booking
ReminderReminds people before their booking24 hours before (you choose)
Follow-upChecks in with people who went quietAfter a number of quiet days

Switch each one on or off on the Agents page. Deleting one of the four switches its setting off. Add back brings it back as it started.

Important: while the Receptionist is on, everyone who messages you gets an answer. If you only want some things answered, switch it off and build agents for those things.

When an agent answers

For every message someone sends you, in a chat with one person:

  1. If an agent asked them a question, their message is the answer and that agent carries on.
  2. If they’re waiting in your Inbox after a handover, agents that answer anything leave them alone (see Your Inbox).
  3. Someone messaging for the first time gets your welcome agent, if you have one.
  4. Then the agents that start on a message are tried, oldest first. The first one that takes it answers, and only that one.
  5. If none took it, the Receptionist answers (if it’s on). If nothing is on, nothing is sent.

A few things always hold:

  • One reply per message. Two agents never both answer the same message.
  • Agents never answer in groups with AI, and never read photos, voice notes or files as questions. Those come to your Inbox.
  • Anyone you set to Only you reply is never answered by an agent.
  • If a chat gets very fast back and forth (6 replies in 3 minutes, or 10 in 10), agents stop for that chat and it comes to your Inbox. That stops two bots answering each other.

Making an agent

On Agents, describe what you want (“when someone asks how to pay, send my account details”) and press Build, start from a template, or start from scratch.

When to use it

  • By what they mean. Write one sentence (“someone wants to order something”) and, if you like, examples of what people send. Any message that means it, in any words or language, is picked. Use whole phrases as examples, not single words: an example that appears word for word in a message is taken straight away.
  • On something exact: certain words (has any of, is exactly, starts with), a photo, voice note or file, someone new, before or after a booking, someone going quiet, another agent handing it over, or nothing else took it.
  • Sent by Me. Words you type yourself, like /hours, can start an agent. You choose whether your message stays, is deleted, or turns into the reply. A / in front keeps it from going off in an ordinary message.
  • When it works (days and hours) and how often (every time, or once an hour, a day, a week per person). Outside those, the agent is skipped and the message goes on to the next one, often the Receptionist.

Steps

InstructionsPlain words for the AI. With no tools it answers once from what it knows. @mention tools (free times, book, move or cancel, send later, label) or something to find out (“their email”) and it holds a conversation until it’s done.

Knowledge, rules and handing over

  • What it knows: all of Knowledge, some topics, or only its own notes.
  • Rules: your own lines it follows (“never offer Sundays”). It never makes up a price, a time or a fact, whatever the rules say.
  • Hand over to me when: it always hands over when someone asks for a person. When it doesn’t know, it asks “want me to pass this on?” and only a yes comes to you, or you can choose to hand over straight away. Add your own cases in plain words.
  • Try it and Tests run the agent with the real AI and your real free times, and send nothing. Try it doesn’t show whether the agent would be picked for a message. Only a real message tests that.

Your Inbox

Everything handed to you is in the Inbox: the conversation that led to it and why (“Didn’t know the answer”, “Asked for a person”, “Sent a voice note”).

  • Handle in WhatsApp opens their chat on your phone. You reply there, as you always have.
  • Done when it’s sorted. Undo if you pressed it by mistake.
  • Quiet after a handover. While someone is waiting in your Inbox, agents that answer anything leave them alone, whatever they send, for 12 hours or until you press Done. Change it in Settings, AI replies, from an hour to a week or until Done. Agents on exact words still answer.
  • Agents reply / Only you reply on each person. Only you reply means agents never answer them.
  • Request payment sends a payment link when Stripe or Paystack is connected.

When you reply yourself

Anything you send in a chat, from your phone or anywhere else (text, a voice note, a photo, a sticker, even a reaction), tells agents you’re there. Agents that answer anything stay quiet in that chat for your Pause after you reply time. Agents on exact words still answer, because that is what the person asked for. You can turn off “steps back while you’re chatting” for any agent.

Wait before replying gives you a few seconds to answer first before the Receptionist does.

Bookings

  • Services: what can be booked, how long it takes, its price, and its own questions.
  • Availability: weekly hours with breaks, time off, and rules like notice needed and bookings per day. Time off asks what to do about anyone already booked.
  • The agenda: today first, every booking with who added it. Add a booking by hand, move it (reminders move too), or cancel it with the message shown first.
  • Didn’t come? only offers a short “sorry we missed you” message in your words.
  • Two people can never be booked into the same time. All times are in your time zone.

Messages

  • Schedule a message to a person, a label or a group, once or repeating every day, week or month. Names fill themselves in.
  • Broadcast sends to many people, carefully: only people who have messaged you, one at a time with gaps, between 8am and 8pm, at most 50 a day, and never to anyone who replied STOP. A list of 50 takes about an hour. This is on purpose: sending fast is what gets numbers flagged.
  • Everything waiting, sent or not sent is listed with the reason, and can be stopped.

Connected apps

  • Google Calendar: bookings go into your calendar, and your busy times block bookings.
  • Apple Calendar and Outlook: a private calendar link that shows your bookings. Your calendar app refreshes it when it chooses.
  • Stripe and Paystack: make payment links from a chat, an agent or the assistant, and see when they’re paid. The money goes straight to your account. Kavedi never holds it or sees a card.
  • Apps shown as Coming soon don’t connect yet.

The assistant

The face on the right (or in the middle of the tab bar on a phone) is an assistant that knows your account. Ask it things (“who’s booked tomorrow?”) or tell it to do them (“make an agent that sends my menu when someone asks for it”, “take Friday off”). Settings change at once with Undo. Anything that messages people, adds an agent or deletes something waits for your tap. You can talk to it with the microphone and attach files.

Limits

  • AI credits. Every AI answer uses credits (1 credit is 1,000 tokens). During the beta it’s free, with 30 credits an hour per account, which is about 15 replies. Over it, messages come to your Inbox as “Hourly AI limit reached” until the hour is up. See it in Settings, Plan & billing.
  • An agent can have up to 20 steps. Up to 3 groups can be picked across all agents.
  • Broadcasts: 50 messages a day, 8am to 8pm.

Keeping your number safe

  • Kavedi only replies to people who messaged you first, and never starts a new chat on its own.
  • It isn’t shown online all day, and it doesn’t type before automatic messages.
  • Your number is only ever connected once. Two copies fighting over one number is a warning sign to WhatsApp, so Kavedi prevents it.
  • Like any tool that links to WhatsApp this way, there is some risk. The terms explain it.

Problems and fixes

  • It answers everything. The Receptionist is on. Switch it off on Agents, or set people to Only you reply.
  • An agent didn’t answer. Check it’s on, inside its hours, not inside its “how often” wait, and that you didn’t reply in that chat a moment before. If the person is waiting in your Inbox, press Done.
  • The pairing code didn’t work. Codes expire after a few minutes. Ask for a new one, and type it under Link with phone number instead.
  • WhatsApp disconnected. Settings, WhatsApp shows why. If you removed the linked device on your phone, connect again.
