Build agents

Steps

Steps are what an agent does, in order. There are eight kinds.

StepWhat it does
InstructionsPlain words for the AI to carry out. See below.
SayExactly your words, with a picture, file, sticker or reaction if you like.
AskAsks a question, waits for their reply and keeps it. Can offer a numbered list to pick from.
IfDoes one thing or another, depending on what they said, a label, or chance.
DoOne action: look up free times, book, move or cancel, send later, label, payment link, get something from a web address, wait, tell me.
RunRuns another agent’s steps here.
Hand overTo you (it lands in your Inbox) or to another agent.
EndStops, with last words or none.

Instructions#

Write what you want in plain words: “Answer their question from what you know.” With nothing else, it answers once from your Knowledge.

Type @ to add tools: free times, book, move or cancel, send later, label. Name something to find out, like their email, and it asks for it. With tools or something to find out, it holds a conversation until the job is done.

When it can’t says what happens if it gets stuck: hand over to you, carry on to the next step, or run steps you choose.

Limits#

  • Up to 20 steps, counting the ones inside an If.
  • Up to 3 Ask steps.
  • An If can’t have another If inside it.
