Build agents

Hand over to me when

When an agent sends a chat to you instead of answering.

CaseWhat happens
They ask for a personAlways comes to your Inbox at once. This can’t be switched off.
It doesn’t knowIt asks “want me to pass this on?” and only a yes comes to you. Or choose Hand over straight away.
Your own casesWrite them in plain words, like “they want a refund”. Any one that’s true hands it over, and your Inbox says which.

Hellos and small talk#

By default an agent stays quiet for messages that ask nothing: “good morning”, “thanks”, “ok”, an emoji. They aren’t answered and don’t come to your Inbox. A greeting with a question in it still gets an answer.

Choose Reply if you want it to say hello back and answer chit-chat.

What they’re told#

When a chat comes to you, Kavedi sends your holding message, or nothing if it’s blank. Change it in Settings, AI replies, When a chat comes to you.
