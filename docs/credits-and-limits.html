Credits and limits

What AI answers cost, and the limits that keep things safe.

Credits#

Every AI answer uses credits. One credit is 1,000 tokens, roughly a short reply. During the beta it’s free, with up to 30 credits an hour per account, about 15 replies.

Over the limit, messages come to your Inbox as “Hourly AI limit reached” until the hour is up. See your use in Settings, Plan and billing.
