Getting started

Quickstart

Set up Kavedi and get your first automatic answer. It takes about ten minutes.

  1. Sign in

    Go to kavedi.com/sign-in and continue with Google or your email. During the beta your email has to be let in first.

  2. Answer the welcome

    Kavedi asks your name or your business’s, what you do, and the questions people ask you most. Each step can be skipped, and everything can be changed later.

  3. Connect WhatsApp

    Enter your number and type the pairing code into WhatsApp on your phone. See Connect WhatsApp.

  4. Teach your answers

    In Knowledge, type the things you tell people every day: prices, opening hours, how to pay, where you are.

  5. Switch on an agent

    On Agents, switch on the Receptionist to answer questions, or build your own agent for just one job.

  6. Test it

    From another phone, message your number with a question you taught. The answer arrives in seconds.

The Setup button at the top (or the card on Today on a phone) shows what’s left to do.
