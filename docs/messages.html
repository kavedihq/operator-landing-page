Using Kavedi

Messages

Schedule a message, or send one to many people, carefully.

Schedule a message#

To a person, a label or a group, once or repeating every day, week or month. Names fill themselves in: {firstname} becomes their first name.

Broadcast#

Send to many people at once, with rails that protect your number:

  • Only people who have messaged you.
  • One at a time, with gaps, in small bursts.
  • Between 8am and 8pm.
  • At most 50 a day.
  • Never to anyone who replied STOP.

A list of 50 takes about an hour. That’s on purpose: sending fast is what gets numbers flagged.

Going out#

Everything waiting, sent or not sent is listed with the reason, and can be stopped.
