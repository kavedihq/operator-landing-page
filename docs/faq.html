FAQ

Questions people ask before and after they start.

Is this WhatsApp Business API?#

No. Kavedi links to your existing number as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. No Meta business verification needed.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?#

Usually, yes: the regular WhatsApp app and the WhatsApp Business app both work. A number already connected to Meta’s official Business API can’t be linked.

Will it make up prices?#

No. It only answers from what you taught it. If it doesn’t know, it says so and offers to pass it on.

Does it understand Pidgin and other languages?#

Yes. It replies in the language people write in.

Can I still use WhatsApp myself?#

Yes. Your phone works as normal, and agents step back when you reply.

Does it read photos and voice notes?#

No. They come to your Inbox. Voice notes can be transcribed there with one tap.

What does Kavedi keep?#

Message text for 2 days, encrypted, and handed-over conversations for 90 days. Never photos, voice notes or files. See the privacy policy.

How much does it cost?#

It’s free during the beta. See pricing for the plans after.
