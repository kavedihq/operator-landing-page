Your WhatsApp number

Kavedi connects to your WhatsApp number as a linked device, the way WhatsApp Web does. It uses WhatsApp’s multi-device connection, not the official WhatsApp Business API, and Kavedi is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp or Meta.

Because WhatsApp doesn’t offer this connection officially for automation, a number that is connected carries some risk of being flagged, restricted or banned by WhatsApp. That risk sits with your number, not with Kavedi, and WhatsApp’s decisions about your account are outside our control.

Kavedi is built to keep that risk low: it only replies to people who wrote to you first, reads only the groups you pick, sends anything outbound one message at a time between 8am and 8pm and at most 50 a day, never sends to someone who replied STOP, and asks you before messaging someone you’ve never chatted with.

What we recommend: connect a work number rather than the only number your family can reach you on if you have one; keep broadcasts to people who expect to hear from you; and if WhatsApp restricts your number, don’t link or unlink devices until it lifts — restrictions usually lift within hours.
