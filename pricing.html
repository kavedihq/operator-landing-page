Questions about paying

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel in Settings, Plan & billing. You keep your plan until the end of what you paid for, then move to Free. Your agents and answers stay.

Do I pay anything during the free trial?

No. You add a card to start the trial and the first payment is taken when it ends: after 14 days on yearly, 7 on monthly. We remind you before then, and if you cancel first you pay nothing.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days of any payment and we refund all of it. Email support@kavedi.com from the address you signed up with.

What currency will I pay in?

US dollars. Your bank shows what left your account in your own currency.

What if I run out of AI replies?

Nothing stops. Messages it would have answered come to your inbox instead, and you’re never charged extra. Move up a plan whenever you like, or wait for next month.

Can I change plans later?

Yes, up or down at any time. Moving up starts straight away. Moving down starts when the time you paid for ends.

Is it free right now?

Yes. Every plan is free while Kavedi is in beta, and we’ll tell you before anything costs money. The first 10 testers get two months of Pro free when pricing starts.

Is my card safe?

Yes. Payments are taken by a secure payment provider, and Kavedi never sees or keeps your card details.
