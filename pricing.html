Pricing

Simple, flat pricing.

One price a month, in your currency. Nothing per message, and no surprise bills.

Kavedi is in private beta, and testers use it free. These prices are what we expect to charge after launch, and can still change.

Free

$0 / month

Enough to connect your number and see it answer your real messages.

  • 1 WhatsApp number
  • 100 AI replies a month
  • Teach it by typing, or upload a price list
  • Hands you anything it wasn’t taught
  • Reply from Kavedi, and exceptions
  • Scheduled messages, 30 a month

Pro

Most people

$9 / month

For when Kavedi is doing real work every day.

  • Everything in Free
  • 1,500 AI replies a month
  • Appointment booking in the chat
  • Reminders and check-ins
  • Scheduled messages, no limit
  • Broadcasts to people and groups
  • Word-for-word answers

Business

$24 / month

Busy numbers, and help getting it right from day one.

  • Everything in Pro
  • 5,000 AI replies a month
  • Voice notes transcribed on request
  • Set up with you, with your answers written from your documents
  • Priority support

An AI reply is one message Kavedi reads and answers with AI. Scheduled messages, reminders, broadcasts and replies you send yourself use none. Run out and Kavedi doesn’t stop: it passes messages to you instead of answering, and never bills you for more.

Use it free while it’s in beta.

The first 10 testers get two months of Pro free when pricing starts.
