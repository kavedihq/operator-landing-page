      Is anyone reading my chats?

      No. Message text is kept encrypted for 2 days, then deleted. Only conversations handed to you are kept, for 90 days. The privacy policy has the details.

      Can I stop it?

      Any time. Every agent has its own switch, and disconnecting removes Kavedi from your number entirely.
