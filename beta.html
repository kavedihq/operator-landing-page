      It connects like WhatsApp Web

      Kavedi is a linked device on the number you already use, and you keep using WhatsApp as normal. It isn’t affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

      There is some risk

      WhatsApp doesn’t offer this officially, so a linked device carries some risk. Kavedi only replies to people who wrote first, reads only the groups you pick, sends slowly, and sends nothing overnight.

      Questions

      Something else? Email support@kavedi.com.ng.

      Where does it work?

      Anywhere WhatsApp works. It replies in the language people write to you in, and runs on your time zone.

      What does it cost?

      Nothing while you’re testing. The pricing page shows what it will cost afterwards. Those prices can still change before launch.

      How long does the beta last?

      As long as it takes to get right. We ask testers for two weeks of real use, and you’re welcome to keep going after that.

      What happens to my messages?

      Kavedi reads a message to act on it and doesn’t keep it. The only message text stored is a question it passed to you. The privacy policy has the details.

      Can I stop it?

      Any time. Every trigger, replies, bookings and check-ins each have their own switch, and disconnecting removes Kavedi from your number entirely.
