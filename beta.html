There is some risk

WhatsApp doesn’t offer this officially, so a linked device carries some risk. Kavedi only answers people who wrote first, paces everything it sends, and sends nothing overnight.

Questions

Where does it work?

Anywhere WhatsApp works. It replies in the language people write to you in, and runs on your own time zone.

What does it cost?

Nothing while you’re testing. The pricing page shows what it’ll cost after, and those prices are indicative until launch.

How long does the beta last?

As long as it takes to get right. We ask testers for two weeks of real use, and you’re welcome to keep going after that.

What happens to my messages?

Kavedi reads a message to answer it and doesn’t keep it. The only message text stored is a question it passed to you. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I stop it?

Any time. Replies, bookings and check-ins are three switches, and disconnecting removes Kavedi from your number entirely.
