Privacy Policy

Last updated September 6, 2026
In plain language
  • No person reads your chats — the AI writes the replies. Our team only opens a conversation if you ask for support, or if something breaks.
  • We store what Kavedi needs to keep working: your connection, your messages, and whatever you’ve taught it.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we don’t need your WhatsApp password.
  • Disconnect any time. Ask us to delete your data and we will.

Kavedi is an AI that connects to a WhatsApp number you already use, so it can answer messages on your behalf. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you stay in control of it. Kavedi is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

What Kavedi connects to

Kavedi links to your existing WhatsApp number using an unofficial multi-device connection method (the same approach WhatsApp Web uses), not the official WhatsApp Business API. To keep that connection alive, we hold the session and authentication state for your linked number on our servers. We never ask for or store your WhatsApp account password, and we never collect payment credentials through this connection.

What we collect

  • Your "knowledge": the text, PDFs, images and product or business information you feed Kavedi so it can answer accurately.
  • Conversation data: the WhatsApp messages Kavedi needs to see to generate replies, and the replies it sends.
  • Session and authentication state: the credentials that keep your WhatsApp connection linked, held on your behalf.
  • Waitlist emails: the email address you give us to join the waitlist.

Why we collect it

We use this data only to operate Kavedi: to keep your WhatsApp connection active, to generate replies grounded in the knowledge you've given it, to flag conversations that need you, and to run the waitlist and tell you when it's your turn.

How it's stored and secured

Your data is encrypted in transit and stored on access-controlled infrastructure. Session and authentication state is treated as sensitive: it is scoped to your account and used only to maintain your WhatsApp connection.

Who can access it

Access is limited to the Kavedi team, and only as needed to operate, support, or debug the service. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Your choices

You can disconnect your WhatsApp number from Kavedi at any time, and you can ask us to delete your data — including your knowledge base, conversation history, and session state. Email support@kavedi.com.ng and we'll action it.

Changes to this policy

Kavedi is early-stage and this policy will change as the product does. We'll update the date above when it does.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: support@kavedi.com.ng
