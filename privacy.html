Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026
In plain language
  • Kavedi keeps the text of your chats for 2 days, encrypted, and then deletes it.
  • When a conversation is handed over to you, a copy of that one conversation is kept for 90 days, encrypted, so you can read it in your inbox.
  • Photos, voice notes, videos and files people send are never stored.
  • We keep what you set up: what you taught it, your agents, bookings, contacts (encrypted) and what’s queued to go out.
  • No person reads your chats. Our team only looks if you ask for help, or to fix a fault.
  • Messages are sent to AI providers to be answered. They don’t get your contacts or your account.
  • Disconnect any time. Delete your account and everything goes with it.

Kavedi connects to a WhatsApp number you already use and does what you set it up to do on your behalf: agents that reply, book and hand chats over to you, reminders, follow-ups and messages you schedule. This policy says exactly what is stored, where it goes, how long it stays and how to get rid of it. Kavedi is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

How Kavedi connects

Kavedi links to your number as a companion device, the same way WhatsApp Web does. It is not the official WhatsApp Business API. To hold that link we store the session and authentication state for your number, encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key that is not kept in the database. We never ask for your WhatsApp password, because there isn’t one to give.

Messages and conversations

  • Every message in a one-to-one chat, for 2 days. Its text (or a caption), who sent it (them, you, or which part of Kavedi), what kind of message it was and when. The text is encrypted. This short buffer is what lets Kavedi keep a copy of a conversation if it is handed to you, and reply to the exact message someone sent. After 2 days it is deleted automatically.
  • Conversations handed over to you, for 90 days. When an agent hands a chat to you (it didn’t know the answer, or one of your “hand over to me when” cases came true), we keep an encrypted copy of that conversation and why it was handed over, so it shows in your inbox. It goes after 90 days, or with your account.
  • Never kept: photos, voice notes, videos, documents, stickers and locations people send. For a voice note waiting for you, we keep only a reference that lets Kavedi fetch it from WhatsApp again if you press Transcribe, and the transcript once you do.
  • A conversation an agent is in the middle of (it asked a question and is waiting for the reply) is kept encrypted until it finishes, or for an hour at most.
  • Groups, statuses, broadcast lists and Channels are not read, unless you pick a group for an agent (up to three). Messages in a group you picked are checked against your agents and not stored. Every other group is dropped before it is even decrypted. Kavedi never replies in a group with AI.

What you set up

  • What you teach it: the answers you write or say, and the text read out of any price list, flyer or document you upload. The file itself is not kept, only the answers you approve. Each answer also gets a numerical “embedding” so the right one can be found, and a count of how often it was used.
  • Your agents: their names, when they run, their steps, rules, notes, “hand over to me when” cases and the test conversations you write for them, plus any picture, file or sticker you give them to send (kept in private storage until no agent uses it). Each time an agent runs we record when, for which chat, and whether it worked, not what was said.
  • Your contacts: names, numbers and when each chat last had a message, from the address book WhatsApp syncs to the linked device. Stored encrypted. Labels you add, and anyone who replied STOP, are stored too.
  • Bookings: the time, the service, the name of whoever booked, their answers to the service’s questions, who added it, and whether they came. Your hours, breaks, time off and rules.
  • Messages you schedule or broadcast: the text, who it goes to, when and how often it repeats, until it is sent, and afterwards whether it was delivered or read.
  • Settings and profile: your name, what you do, address, phone, website, time zone, and how you want replies written.
  • Your chats with the assistant inside the app, including what it read from a file you added to a question. Encrypted, and deleted 90 days after you last opened them, or when you delete them.
  • Dictation: what you say into the microphone is sent to be turned into text and is not kept.
  • Counts, not content: one row per reply or hand-over (the kind and who it involved), and how much AI each account used per day (tokens and calls, never the words). That is where “9 replies today” and “AI this month” come from.
  • Notifications: if you switch them on, the address your browser gives us for push notifications, and your email for email ones.
  • Your account: the email you sign in with, and your name and picture if you use Google.
  • Beta applications: your email, and whatever you chose to tell us (your name, what you do, where you are, how you use WhatsApp), plus whether we let you in.

Apps you connect

  • Google Calendar. If you sign in with Google to connect it, we keep an encrypted token that lets Kavedi add, move and remove your bookings as events in your calendar, and read when you’re busy so nobody is booked then. The assistant can read your events to answer you. Your events are read when needed and not stored. Disconnecting, or deleting your account, gives the access back to Google.
  • The calendar link (Apple Calendar, Outlook): a private address that shows your bookings to the calendar you add it to. It shows names and services, never phone numbers. Anyone with the link can see it, so you can replace it with a new one at any time.
  • Stripe and Paystack. The secret key you paste is stored encrypted. For each payment link we keep the amount, what it was for, who it was sent to and whether it was paid. Kavedi never holds the money and never sees a card. Disconnecting removes the key, and the notifications Kavedi set up on your Stripe account.

Who else sees it

To answer a message, its text is sent to an AI provider along with the answers you taught Kavedi, the agent’s instructions and your profile. To find the right answer, the message is also turned into an embedding. Voice notes you ask to transcribe, and what you dictate, go to a speech-to-text provider. None of them receive your contact list, your account or your other conversations. Today that means Groq (replies, agents, the assistant, transcription) and Google Gemini (embeddings, and reading uploaded documents). Providers can change, and this page will say so.

Kavedi runs on Render, with data stored by Supabase (Postgres, in Frankfurt, Germany). Email goes through Resend. Beta applications are stored with Kavedi’s own data, or go through Web3Forms if that is briefly unavailable. This site is served through Cloudflare. Apps you connect (Google, Stripe, Paystack) get only what is described above. We do not sell anything to anyone, and there is no advertising.

An agent step that gets something from the internet fetches the web address you typed, from Kavedi’s server. If you put someone’s words in that address, those words go to that site, and nothing else about you or them does.

Worth knowing while Kavedi is free: we use free tiers of those AI providers, and some providers reserve the right to review content sent on a free tier to improve their services. If that matters for what people send you, ask us where we are before connecting a number.

Who at Kavedi can see it

Access is limited to the Kavedi team, and only to run the service, support you, or fix something. Nobody reads your chats for any other reason. Message text is encrypted, and most of it is gone after 2 days.

The people who message you

They are messaging you, not us, and their messages stay in your WhatsApp. Kavedi keeps their messages as described above: 2 days, or 90 days for a conversation handed over to you. If someone replies STOP, that is stored permanently so nothing automatic reaches them again. If someone asks you to remove their details, email us and we will.

How long we keep it

  • Message text: 2 days.
  • Conversations handed over to you: 90 days.
  • Chats with the assistant: 90 days after you last opened them.
  • A conversation an agent is waiting in: until it finishes, an hour at most.
  • Records of agent runs: 30 days.
  • What you taught it, your agents, contacts, bookings, settings and connected apps: until you delete them or your account.
  • Scheduled messages: kept after sending, so you can see what went out.
  • Server logs: no message text, only counts, numbers and errors, kept by our hosting provider for a short period.

Your choices

Disconnect your number at any time in Settings → WhatsApp: Kavedi unlinks the device and forgets the stored login and address book. Delete account in Settings removes everything above, gives back access to Google and removes what Kavedi set up on Stripe, and cannot be undone. You can also email support@kavedi.com to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion.

Where you stand legally

Wherever you are, the arrangement is the same: for the people messaging you, you are in control of their data (the “controller”), and Kavedi processes it on your behalf. That is how the UK and EU GDPR describe it, as do Nigeria’s Data Protection Act and similar laws elsewhere. Your data is processed by the providers named above, who may be outside your country. You can ask us to hand back or delete what we hold at any time, and we’ll sign a processing agreement if you need one.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your WhatsApp session, message text, handed-over conversations, contacts, assistant chats and the keys of apps you connect are also encrypted at rest, with a key held outside the database. Every request to Kavedi is checked against your signed-in account, so one account can never read another’s. If something goes wrong that affects you, we’ll tell you.

Changes to this policy

Kavedi is early, and this will change as the product does. The date at the top changes with it, and anything material will be sent to you.
