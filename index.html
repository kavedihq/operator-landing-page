What it does, and what it won’t.

What Kavedi does for you.

AI auto-reply
It answers the questions you get asked constantly, in your tone, using what you’ve told it — and flags anything it’s not sure about for you to take over.
Instant replies Your knowledge Flags uncertainty
Appointment booking
It fits right into the conversation you’re already having. Someone asks if you’re free Thursday, Kavedi checks what’s actually open and offers a real slot — no separate app, no link to click. They confirm right there in the chat, and it’s on the calendar.
In-chat Calendar sync Confirmations
Scheduled follow-ups
Write the message once, and it goes out when it’s supposed to — a payment reminder, a check-in, the follow-up you meant to send and forgot. Every message still goes out one at a time, to the person it’s meant for. It’s not bulk sending, just the message you’d have sent yourself, on time.
Payment nudges Check-ins Paced sending
Reminders
The nudge before an appointment. The nudge on the day something’s due. Kavedi sends them automatically, so remembering isn’t on you anymore.
Appointments Due dates Automatic
Your team, one number
More than one person can answer the same number, each from their own login. Kavedi keeps track of who’s already replied, so nobody double-replies to the same person or steps on a conversation someone else is already handling.
Shared inbox No double-replies Handoff-aware
