Getting started

Three steps, and in the beta we do them with you.

  1. Step 1

    Connect your number

    Type a code into WhatsApp on your phone. Kavedi links the way WhatsApp Web does, and your chats stay where they are.

  2. Step 2

    Teach it your answers

    Prices, hours, how to book. Type them in, or upload the price list or flyer you already have and check what it read.

  3. Step 3

    Switch on what you want

    Replies, bookings and check-ins each have their own switch. Turn any of them off whenever you like.

Straight answers

What it won’t do, and who’s behind it.

Kelvin A., Ada D., Abdulwakil O. and Chidera E. build Kavedi. Write to support@kavedi.com.ng and one of us replies.

  • It won’t guess

    No price, time or rule you didn’t give it. If it wasn’t taught the answer, it says nothing and passes the message to you.

  • It won’t pretend to be you

    Asked outright, it says it’s an assistant. It won’t agree to a discount, a delivery or a date you didn’t set.

  • It doesn’t keep your chats

    It reads a message to answer it and doesn’t store it. What it keeps: the questions it passes to you, what you taught it, and your contacts, encrypted.

  • It stops when you say

    Every part has its own switch. Disconnecting removes Kavedi from your number, and we delete your data when you ask.

  • The risk, plainly

    WhatsApp doesn’t offer this officially, so a linked device carries some risk. Kavedi only answers people who wrote first, sends anything outbound slowly and only between 8am and 8pm, and warns you before messaging someone new. Use a work number if you have one.

Questions

Something else? Email support@kavedi.com.ng.

Is my number safe?

There’s a real risk and we won’t pretend otherwise. Kavedi links to your number the way WhatsApp Web does, and WhatsApp doesn’t offer this officially. So Kavedi only replies to people who wrote to you first, sends anything outbound one message at a time between 8am and 8pm, up to 50 a day, and warns you before it messages someone you’ve never chatted with. Use a work number rather than your personal one if you can.

What happens when it doesn’t know the answer?

It says nothing. The question turns up in Kavedi with the person’s name, and you answer it there or on your phone. After that, Kavedi stays out of that chat for a while. One tap turns your reply into an answer, so the next person gets it straight away.

If you’d rather it said “let me check” in the meantime, write that line once in Settings.

Will it sound like a bot?

It writes each reply from what you taught it, in the tone you pick: friendly, short or formal. Anything that must go out exactly as written, like account details, you mark word for word. Asked outright whether it’s a person, it says it’s an assistant.

Does it work where I am?

Anywhere WhatsApp works. It replies in the language people write to you in, times follow your time zone, and prices show in your currency.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Most numbers link fine. Some Business accounts that are tied to Meta’s Business Platform don’t link yet. Tell us which WhatsApp you use when you apply, and we’ll check before you connect anything.

Is anyone reading my chats?

No. Kavedi doesn’t keep conversations. It keeps the questions it passes to you, what you taught it, and your contacts, encrypted. The privacy policy lists everything.
