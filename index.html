Questions?

Do I need a new number?

No. Kavedi links to the number you already use, the same way WhatsApp Web does. Your phone keeps working as normal.

Will it make things up?

No. Agents only say what you taught them. Anything else, like a refund or a question they can’t answer, comes to your inbox.

Can I still reply myself?

Yes, in WhatsApp as always. When you reply, the agents step back from that chat.

Is anyone reading my chats?

No. Message text is kept encrypted for 2 days, then deleted. Only conversations handed to you are kept, for 90 days.

What does it cost?

Nothing during the beta. We’ll tell you before anything changes.

Is it the official WhatsApp app?

No. It connects as a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. Kavedi isn’t affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
