What connecting actually means.

Same number, nothing moves

Kavedi connects to the WhatsApp number you already use, like WhatsApp Web. Your chats stay right where they are.

You see everything, always

Every conversation shows up in your dashboard, and you can take over any chat in one tap. Nothing is hidden from you.

No person is reading your chats

Replies are written by the AI. Our team only opens a conversation if you ask for help, or if something breaks.

Leave whenever

Disconnect any time and Kavedi stops replying immediately. No lock-in, no contract — and you can ask us to delete your data on the way out.
